# Homepage design goals

1. Preserve the approved white/purple EU Solution Foundry visual language.
2. Make Smart Search the dominant interaction.
3. Keep the opportunity product card compact and evidence-rich.
4. Keep human problems as the discovery vocabulary, never generic categories.
5. Keep Greek-market signals visually separate from global/AliExpress supply signals.
6. Keep B2B visible but secondary to consumer discovery.
7. Maintain clear CORE vs LAB semantics.
8. Avoid fake trend percentages, fake discounts and generic social proof.
