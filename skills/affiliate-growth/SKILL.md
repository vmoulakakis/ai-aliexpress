# Affiliate Growth

## Commercial objective
Maximize **qualified purchase decisions**, not raw outbound clicks.

A click generated by misleading urgency, weak relevance or hidden commercial intent is a product-quality failure even if CTR rises.

## Trust rules
- Clearly disclose affiliate relationships close to the first meaningful product CTA.
- Use plain Greek, not legalistic fine print.
- Do not imply that NHMA is the merchant.
- Do not invent scarcity, countdowns, “only X left”, fake discounts or guaranteed savings.
- Do not claim “best” without a defined evidence basis.
- Affiliate compensation must not influence relevance ranking.
- Outbound affiliate links use `rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"`.

## Added-value rule
NHMA must add value beyond merchant feed text:
- interpret human needs and constraints
- filter bad candidates
- explain trade-offs
- compare options
- identify uncertainty
- organize use-case/problem solutions
- remember prior requirements

Do not publish thin pages that simply restate merchant titles/descriptions plus affiliate links.

## Funnel event taxonomy
Instrument at least:
- `search_started`
- `search_clarification_shown`
- `search_completed`
- `zero_result`
- `constraint_relaxed`
- `result_impression`
- `product_opened`
- `affiliate_click`
- `compare_added`
- `compare_viewed`
- `saved_product`
- `chat_opened`
- `chat_message_sent`
- `chat_triggered_search`
- `photo_search_started`

Attach anonymous session id, product id where applicable, query class, result count, rank and surface (`search|chat|compare`). Never log provider secrets or unnecessary personal data.

## KPI hierarchy
Primary quality metrics:
1. clean-search success rate
2. wrong-category / accessory false-positive rate
3. hard-constraint violation rate
4. zero-result recovery rate
5. compare/save engagement
6. qualified affiliate CTR
7. downstream conversion/commission when network data is available
8. repeat-session usefulness

Guardrails: complaint rate, rapid back-clicks, irrelevant-click rate, latency and fabricated-claim rate.

## Experimentation
One hypothesis at a time. Define:
- target metric
- guardrail metric
- population
- minimum observation window/sample requirement
- stop condition

Examples worth testing:
- “Γιατί ταιριάζει” placement
- comparison CTA placement
- qualitative match labels
- compact vs expanded evidence

Do not A/B test deception.

## Content / SEO
Prioritize original problem-solving assets:
- buyer decision guides
- comparisons with explicit trade-offs
- compatibility checklists
- price/feature explainers
- seasonal problem pages grounded in real user demand
- downloadable planning/checklist tools where useful

Avoid mass-generated near-duplicate “best X” pages. Editorial affiliate pages must stand alone as useful content even if every affiliate link were removed.
