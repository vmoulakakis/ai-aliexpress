# Growth Marketing

## Positioning
NHMA is not “another AliExpress shop”. Market the outcome:
**“Πες τι χρειάζεσαι ή ποιο πρόβλημα θέλεις να λύσεις — το NHMA σε βοηθά να ξεχωρίσεις τις επιλογές.”**

Lead with problem-solving, decision confidence and time saved. Affiliate mechanics are a disclosure requirement, not the hero message.

## Demand workflow
1. Collect observed demand signals: internal search events, zero-result queries, recurring use cases, seasonal timing and available external demand evidence.
2. Cluster by **consumer problem**, not only merchant category.
3. Check supply quality before promoting a theme.
4. Create one useful decision experience per theme: selector, checklist, comparison, guide or curated workflow.
5. Measure whether users reach relevant products and decisions.

## Campaign architecture
Seasonal themes (Back to School, travel, gifts, home setup, etc.) are **skills/campaign modules**, never the core ontology of the site.

A campaign can provide:
- tailored starter queries
- decision checklist
- dedicated filters
- educational advice
- curated bundles only from verified live products
- downloadable planning assets where useful

The global search/chat remains general purpose.

## Content quality
Every marketing asset answers at least one real question:
- what problem does this solve?
- who is it for?
- what trade-offs matter?
- what should I avoid?
- how do I choose between options?

Avoid generic AI listicles and mass-produced “top 10” pages with no original decision framework.

## Retention loops
Useful, non-spammy reasons to return:
- saved shortlist
- compare later
- remembered constraints
- price/availability re-check only when supported
- seasonal checklist/planner
- “continue where I left off” conversation state

## Experiment rules
Experiment on decision friction, not manipulation. Examples:
- starter-query wording
- filter disclosure hierarchy
- comparison CTA
- product evidence density
- chat entrypoint copy

Define success as improved relevant-search completion / qualified product exploration, not merely more clicks.

## Greek market tone
Use clear contemporary Greek, not literal ecommerce machine translation. Greeklish queries are accepted in product understanding, but customer-facing editorial copy should be polished Greek unless the context requires otherwise.
